## Why share-alike

The Dataset improves every time a broker corrects it. Share-alike keeps those improvements in the open — a competitor can use the data, but they cannot close their copy without opening everything built on it. The code stays MIT; the truth stays shared; the moat is the calibration discipline, the measured precision, and the community that keeps both honest.
